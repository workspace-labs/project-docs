What it is

01

A small study timer that runs in the computer's terminal: 25 minutes of work, then a 5-minute break, with a short sound and a one-line message at each change.

Why

02

Phone timers pull the student into the phone. A timer in the terminal they already have open keeps them on the work.

Who it's for

03
One student

start a session with one command and be told when to stop.

How it works today

04
  1. 1

    The student sets a timer on the phone, and ends up in the phone.

How it will work

05
  1. 1

    The student types one command in the terminal and keeps working until the sound.

Who decides

06
Owner

the student, alone.

What it must do

07
M1

The student can start, pause and stop a session.

✓
Check start a session, pause it, start it again, stop it; each prints one line.
M2

It plays a short sound and prints one line when work time or break time ends.

✓
Check start a session with one-minute times; the sound and the line come at each change.

What it will NOT do

08

No windows or app to click: it lives in the terminal.

No accounts, no statistics, no saving of past sessions.

Later

09

None.

Assumptions and risks

10
Assumption

None.

Risk

None.

How we know it worked

11

One command starts a session, and the sound plays at 25 minutes.

Dates and budget

12
Dates

None: no date.

Budget

None: no budget.
